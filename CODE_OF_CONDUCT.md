# Code of Conduct

teslausb-ng is a small community fork built on a budget. The day-to-day
expectations are pragmatic, not formal.

## What we ask of contributors and users

- **Assume good faith.** Bug reports come from people trying to make
  something work, not from people trying to make you look bad. PR review
  comments come from people trying to merge your work, not from people
  trying to gatekeep.
- **Be specific.** "It doesn't work" wastes the time of whoever ends up
  reading the issue. The
  [bug report template](.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/bug_report.yml) asks for
  the exact information we need; please fill it in.
- **Respect the no-hardware-to-test reality.** The maintainer doesn't own
  a Tesla. Patches that need physical hardware verification can sit
  pending without a recurring tester confirming "this fixes it for me on
  my Model Y." That's not stalling — that's how this project survives.
- **No harassment, slurs, or personal attacks** in issues, PRs,
  discussions, or commit messages. This includes targeting upstream
  maintainers, contributors of other forks, or anyone else.
- **No mandatory cloud or paywall dependencies** added to the codebase.
  Optional integrations with opt-in env vars are fine; flipping any
  existing feature behind a sponsor wall is not.

## What we do about problems

- Open an issue or email the maintainer (see GitHub profile).
- The maintainer will respond within a week. Bad-faith behavior leads to
  closed issues, blocked users, and the matter being dropped.
- We won't run a tribunal. This is a small fork; we don't have the
  bandwidth for one.

## Coverage

This document applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, code review
comments, commit messages, and any other space tied to the
[`danusha2345/teslausb-ng`](https://github.com/danusha2345/teslausb-ng)
repository.

By contributing, you agree to follow it.
